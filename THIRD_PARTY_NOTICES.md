## Visual research references

The visual hierarchy and motion research reviewed the public repositories for [Mineradio](https://github.com/XxHuberrr/Mineradio) (GPL-3.0), [Gentelella](https://github.com/ColorlibHQ/gentelella) (MIT), and [Motion Primitives](https://github.com/ibelick/motion-primitives) (MIT). No source files, names, logos, screenshots, or visual assets from these projects are bundled. The application uses an original HTML/CSS/Canvas implementation based on general principles such as content-first hierarchy, staged entrances, ambient motion, and reduced-motion accessibility.

## Public job data

Remote-job discovery can query the public [Jobicy Remote Jobs API](https://github.com/Jobicy/remote-jobs-api), published under the MIT License. The application links users back to original job pages, caches equivalent requests for one hour, and does not redistribute the feed as a competing job aggregator. No Jobicy source code, logo, or visual asset is bundled.

[JobSpy](https://github.com/speedyapply/JobSpy) was reviewed during technical research, but its platform-scraping implementation and source code are not integrated or bundled. The application instead uses public search results, official career pages, and explicitly public APIs.

## Interview-preparation research

The evidence-first interview preparation workflow was informed by the public [Tech Interview Handbook](https://github.com/yangshun/tech-interview-handbook), [Awesome Interview](https://awesome-interview.github.io/Awesome-Interview/), [tips_for_interview](https://github.com/conanhujinming/tips_for_interview), and [Awesome Behavioral Interviews](https://github.com/ashishps1/awesome-behavioral-interviews). No question bank, article, answer, sample response, or source code from these projects is copied or bundled. Practice questions are generated as original exercises from public role trends and are explicitly labeled as predictions rather than current or leaked test questions.
